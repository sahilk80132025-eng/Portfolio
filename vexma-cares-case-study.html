Vexma Cares — Healthcare & Medical

Vexma Technologies' CEO envisioned a new division under Vexma focused on patient-specific healthcare solutions. Many medical companies require 3D-printed products for patented use cases — surgical guides, prosthetics, anatomical models, surgical instruments, and medical device housings.

Figma Illustrator Antigravity

Strategy

The Problem

Medical professionals lacked a streamlined platform to explore and procure specialized 3D-printed medical devices. The core Vexma site was too broad for the highly regulated healthcare sector.

Messy Navigation

The Solution

A dedicated, accessible healthcare vertical enabling seamless exploration of FDA/ISO-compliant 3D-printed medical solutions. Designed from the ground up to speak directly to surgeons and hospitals.
