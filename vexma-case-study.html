VEXMA — Industrial Manufacturing

A full-stack digital manufacturing hero experience designed for industrial engineers and buyers. The platform maps complex additive manufacturing services, highlights ISO/AS9100D compliance, and introduces instant drag-and-drop 3D quotation so quotation workflows are seamless and high-converting.

Research

Problem

Vexma's company website had an old, simple hero section with no interactive way for clients to request quotes — creating friction in the sales/quotation process.

Solution

Redesigned the hero section with an AI-powered "Get Instant Quote" feature — allowing clients to drag-and-drop files for instant 3D printing quotations.

My Role

The existing UX already had SeedMake's AI-powered feature — letting clients upload files and get an instant quote — but it was buried deep in the site. My role was to bring this feature front and center into the hero section, redesigning the old hero into a new, high-converting layout.

Since SeedMake's underlying code had limited customization access, I designed the feature as a popup/modal that wraps SeedMake's existing functionality — allowing the developer to integrate it smoothly within SeedMake's constraints, without needing to rebuild the feature from scratch.
